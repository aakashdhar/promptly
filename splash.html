Talk from any app

Double-tap Control in any app to start talking, and tap it once when you're done (or double-tap and hold while you talk). Promptly types what you say where your cursor is, and can use text you've selected. All of this needs Accessibility permission.

macOS opens System Settings. Turn on Promptly in the list, then come back — Promptly notices on its own. Without it, tap ⌥ Space to start and stop, and your words are copied for ⌘V instead of typed.
