Ten destinations. Each gets its own format.

Dictation types what you said. The nine Craft modes send it to Claude and bring back what that destination actually takes.

  1. Dictation

    Your words typed where your cursor is. Nothing rewritten, no AI call.

    You said: “can you move standup to ten tomorrow, I’ve got the dentist”

    Slack#team

    can you move standup to ten tomorrow, I’ve got the dentist

  2. Prompt

    A rough idea becomes a detailed prompt: goal, context, requirements, constraints, steps, output format, success criteria, assumptions.

    You said: “so for the support dashboard, um, I want it to pull tickets from zendesk every five minutes…”

    Goal
    Build a small internal dashboard that pulls support tickets from Zendesk every five minutes, groups them by product area, and highlights anything waiting more than four hours.
    Success criteria
    A ticket waiting 4 hours and 1 minute is flagged; one at 3 hours 59 is not. No email addresses end up in the database.

  3. Code

    A task brief Claude Code can finish and verify.

    Example. You said: “add the four hour flag to the ticket table and make sure it’s tested”

    Task
    Flag tickets waiting more than four hours in the grouped ticket table.
    Done when
    npm test passes with a case at 4h 01m (flagged) and 3h 59m (not flagged).

  4. Design

    A new web page, or changes to one you’ve built, as a brief for working HTML and CSS.

    Example. You said: “the pricing cards should stack on phones and match the new header”

    Change
    Pricing cards use the header’s type sizes and spacing.
    On phones
    Cards stack in one column below 640 px.
    Keep
    Current copy and prices. Output working HTML and CSS.

  5. Polish

    Paste rough text, speak the change, get it back clean.

    Example. Pasted: “hey so deploy is gonna be late cuz qa found stuff” · You said: “make it calm and clear”

    The deploy is moving: QA found a few issues and we’re fixing them first. I’ll share the new time today.

  6. Email

    Speak the situation, get a ready-to-send email in the tone you pick.

    Example. You said: “tell Priya the invoice will be a week late, keep it warm”

    Subject Invoice for March, a week later than planned

    Hi Priya, a quick heads-up: the March invoice will reach you a week later than we said. Nothing is wrong on our side, it’s just taking longer to close the month. Thanks for bearing with us.

  7. Image

    An image prompt for Nano Banana and ChatGPT, with optional Midjourney flags.

    Example. You said: “coffee on a rainy café window, morning, moody greens”

    A cup of black coffee on a rain-streaked café window ledge, soft morning light, shallow depth of field, muted greens.

    --ar 3:2 --style raw Midjourney flags, optional

  8. Video

    A video prompt for Google Veo 3.1: camera, style, sound and dialogue.

    You said: “a boat leaving the harbour at dawn, slow push in, warm light, gulls”

    A slow push-in on a fishing boat leaving a quiet harbour at dawn, medium shot, golden-hour light with a teal and orange grade, 16:9 at 1080p, gentle ambient sound of gulls and water.

  9. Workflow

    Plain English becomes importable n8n workflow JSON.

    You said: “when someone fills in the typeform add a row to google sheets and post in slack”

    1. Typeform Trigger
    2. Append to Google Sheets
    3. Post to Slack

  10. Harness

    Describe a job, get a loop Claude Code runs until it’s done, with checks, stop conditions and an optional schedule on your Mac.

    You said: “every night fix the failing tests one at a time and never touch the migrations”

    daily 02:00  Pick › Fix › Check › Record

    Stops when all tests pass, a test still fails after 3 tries, or after 25 passes or 2 hours. Never edits db/migrations/.

Set up in three steps.

  1. Install it

    Download the installer and run it. Windows asks you to confirm it once. The steps are below.

    Paste one line into Terminal and it opens straight away, or download the app and allow it once. Both ways are below.

  2. Allow the mic, connect Claude Code

    Give it the microphone, and Accessibility so it can type into other apps. Setup installs Claude Code or signs you in, right there.

Before you download.

The honest parts, up front.

Does it work on Windows or Linux?

No. Promptly is macOS only.

Do I need a Claude subscription?

Craft needs Claude Code on a Pro or Max plan. Promptly uses that plan, so there’s no extra bill and no API key. Dictation works without calling Claude.

Why does macOS warn me the first time?

The app is personally signed rather than notarised by Apple. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. You only do this once. Or install it with the one line in Terminal, which doesn’t need that step.

What permissions does it ask for?

The microphone, to hear you. Accessibility, to type the result into the app you’re using. Nothing else.

Does my audio get uploaded anywhere?

No. Transcription runs on your Mac with the built-in whisper.cpp. Only the text of a Craft request goes to Claude, through your own Claude Code sign-in. Promptly has no servers of its own.

Which languages can I speak?

Over 90. There’s an optional “Best accuracy” model you can download in Settings if you want fewer mistakes.
